# social creative source of truth

Use this page before opening, rebuilding, or handing off any Yum or Patticake social asset.

## current production creative

- `yum-patticake-creative-launch-2026-07-14/` is the active still, carousel, motion, logo-animation, copy, and publishing system.
- `/asset-gallery` is the current review surface. It is synced only from the active launch pack.

Current visual rules are simple: real photography stays unobstructed, baby blue and logo red lead every designed frame, and copy sits in a dedicated field rather than in a sticker, glow, card, or label over the image.

## retired visual packs

- `yum-patticake-social-motion-pack/`, `yum-social-motion-template-2026/`, `yum-social-launch-batch-2026-07/`, and `instagram/` are historical production references only.

When files disagree, use the active launch pack, the asset gallery, and the AI coder design toolkit in that order.
