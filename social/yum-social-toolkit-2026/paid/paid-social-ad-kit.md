# paid social ad kit

Build separate campaigns for local food, catering, gifting, and event intent. Do not collapse yum! and Patticake into one audience or one landing page.

## 1. local restaurant orders

| Objective | Audience | Funnel | Offer | Landing page | CTA | Success metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales or traffic optimized to completed online-order sessions | Adults near each yum! location, site visitors, menu viewers, and recent orderers split by recency | consideration and conversion | Made-from-scratch breakfast, lunch, dinner, bakery, and pickup. **Creative concept:** Craving-first 9:16 video with a location decision and pickup handoff.

**Visual direction:** Real food macro, four-location cue, white/red/blue cards, pickup handoff.

**Video concept:** 12-15 second craving montage ending on the location selector and Order Now.

**Primary text variations:**

1. Lunch is one location choice away. Pick your nearest yum!, check the current menu, and send the order to the kitchen.
2. Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and the bakery case. Start with what sounds good, then choose your Twin Cities kitchen.
3. The drive home gets easier when dinner is already headed to the pickup counter.
4. Four Twin Cities kitchens. One useful answer to what should we eat?

**Headline variations:**

1. Order from your yum!
2. Lunch is handled
3. Pick your kitchen
4. Bring yum! home
5. See the current menu

**Testing notes:** Test daypart, location, craving family, food-first vs handoff-first, and 4:5 vs 9:16. Exclude recent purchasers from prospecting where data allows.

## deadline copy rule

Use a holiday or social deadline only after the owner confirms the exact date, time zone, product route, and inventory or delivery constraint. Store the approval in the campaign folder and set an asset expiry date.

## measurement setup

- One primary conversion event per campaign.
- UTMs on every destination.
- Creative ID must identify brand, audience, concept, hook, format, date, and version.
- Report qualified leads separately from raw form starts.
- Compare 4:5 feed and 9:16 vertical by placement, not as one blended creative result.
- Review frequency, comments, landing-page conversion, and downstream order quality every week.
