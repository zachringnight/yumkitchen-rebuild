# platform publishing guide

This pack is built to stay readable with sound off while leaving room for licensed, platform-native audio at publish time.

## launch-moment priority

- Lead launch morning with `exports/launch-motion-9x16-10s/patticake-launch-day.mp4`.
- Use `patticake-blue-box-arrival` as the launch-afternoon unboxing and first-slice follow-up.
- Use `patticake-bakery-to-nationwide` for the real Yum origin story, press, partners, and founder-led publishing.
- Use `yum-kitchen-to-handoff` to keep the restaurant brand present during the Patticake rollout.
- The full-frame photographs are intentionally text-free. Do not add platform stickers, captions, or logos over them. Put any necessary platform text on the existing baby-blue message scenes or in the post caption.

## organic Instagram mix

- Use `yum-people-behind-the-plate` when the feed needs a branded human moment rather than another product-only or graphic post.
- Keep at least half of organic publishing centered on people, community, kitchen life, guests, and occasions.
- Limit designed conversion assets to roughly one in five posts. Let real photography own the rest of the grid.
- Use one short caption idea. Add a CTA only when there is a real order, inquiry, location, or story destination.
- Confirm usage rights before reusing customer, child, creator, or review imagery from the live account.

## Instagram and Facebook Reels

- Use the 1080x1920 motion master.
- Keep the platform crop at 9:16. Do not auto-fit it into a smaller canvas.
- Add a licensed audio track in Meta's publishing flow. The moving packaging ribbon and scene cuts are the visual beat markers.
- The opening photography stays clear. A single baby-blue cue carries the scene, the yum! mark moves on the red ribbon rail, and the CTA resolves before the final second.

Source: [Meta Reels ads creative guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads)

## Instagram and Facebook feed video

- Use the native 1080x1350 files in `exports/motion-4x5/`; do not letterbox the vertical master.
- Use the 1080x1080 masters in `exports/motion-1x1/` only where a square placement is required.
- The same message hierarchy and final CTA are recomposed for each frame rather than cropped from 9:16.

## TikTok

- Use the 1080x1920 motion master.
- Add a current, rights-cleared track in TikTok rather than baking an expiring trend into the durable master.
- Keep the opening visual unchanged. It introduces the Yum color signature immediately and gets into the food or packaging without a title card.
- Use the on-screen copy as the visual hook. Keep the post caption short and conversational.

Sources: [TikTok Creative Center 3Rs guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/quicktok/online/Creating_Made_Easier/pc/en), [TikTok short-video guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/tiktok-short-video-best-practice)

## YouTube Shorts

- Use the 10-second 1080x1920 cut from `exports/motion-9x16-10s/` for Shorts and Demand Gen placements.
- The 10-second versions preserve every photo beat before the blue editorial close, then hold the action long enough to read.
- Preview the creative in Google Ads before launch to confirm the platform CTA and UI do not cover the final action line.

Sources: [Google Demand Gen asset specifications](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13704860), [Google video ad specifications and safe zones](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13547298)

## static placements

- Feed: 1080x1350
- Square: 1080x1080
- Story: 1080x1920
- Wide: 1200x675

The baby-blue box face, red packaging ribbon, red type, and original photography are preserved across every crop.

## wide video placements

- Use `exports/motion-16x9/` for 1280x720 landscape inventory and web embeds.
- Keep the exported layout intact; it is a native horizontal composition, not a center-crop of the Reel.

## motion carousel stories

- Use `exports/carousel-motion-9x16/` for full-screen stories and Reels.
- Use `exports/carousel-motion-4x5/` for feed video.
- These cuts translate the location, catering, and gifting narratives into timed scenes with one headline per beat and one CTA only at the close.

## Instagram and Facebook carousels

- Upload the cards in numbered filename order. Do not let the platform reorder them.
- Use all six cards for the location sequence and all five cards for every catering and Patticake sequence.
- Keep the cover card as the organic preview. Each sequence moves from one hook to one useful detail per swipe, then one conversion close.
- Use the final-card destination in `carousel-publishing.md` as the post link or profile-link target.
- Do not add extra text boxes, stickers, or cream/black framing over the exported cards. The blue box face and red ribbon are the navigation system.

Instagram currently supports up to 20 photos or videos in one organic carousel, and Meta recommends cohesive imagery with 4:5 supported for carousel creative. These sets stay intentionally shorter so the sequence resolves before the idea gets repetitive.

Sources: [Instagram multiple-photo post guidance](https://help.instagram.com/269314186824048/), [Meta carousel best practices](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/927639917355563), [Instagram image-resolution guidance](https://help.instagram.com/1631821640426723/)
