# yum! and Patticake voice + media reference map

Prepared July 14, 2026 from the newest consolidated toolkits, the captured live-site copy, the owned social archive, and the updated media/UGC handoff.

## working voice lock

### shared voice

- Warm, casual-polished hospitality.
- Short, concrete, helpful, and food-specific.
- Neighborly and practical, never precious or fussy.
- Real food, real people, real locations, and clear next steps.
- Use `yum!` in lowercase in branded copy.
- Use lowercase display headlines. Keep body copy in sentence case and CTAs short.
- Prefer first-person plural support copy and second-person action copy.
- Use the exclamation point selectively; it already belongs to `yum!`.
- No emoji, jargon, stacked punctuation, invented claims, or generic hype.

### yum! Kitchen and Bakery

Everyday food, local utility, and human hospitality. Lead with the meal, bakery case, location, pickup path, catering setup, or team member. The audience should immediately understand what is available, why it is useful, and what to do next.

Anchor language:

- made from scratch since 2005
- fresh and friendly food
- made from scratch with love
- share the love
- four Twin Cities kitchens
- easy to find, easy to park

### Patticake

Occasion-led and personal, with a little more calm and breathing room. Lead with the real cake, note, package, first slice, table, or recipient relationship. Keep gifting warm and specific rather than premium or luxurious.

Anchor language:

- ship a cake
- send cake, not a card
- the note is part of the gift
- birthdays, thank-yous, and the kind of week that needs cake
- yum!'s signature chocolate cake

Do not use `luxury`, `gourmet`, `elevated`, `artisan`, `decadent`, or `premium` as brand positioning.

## creative writing rules

- Headline: 3-8 words, lowercase, one thought.
- Support: one useful proof line, not three equal copy boxes.
- CTA: one action in Title Case.
- Motion: the hook appears immediately, visible proof arrives by second three, and the CTA lands by the final third.
- Captions: concrete food or occasion detail, one human or operational detail, one accurate CTA.
- Community replies: short, public-safe, and routed to the correct owned order, inquiry, menu, location, or contact path.
- Creator voice: first person, specific location or occasion, real reason for choosing the item, and no scripted superlatives.

## visual and media lock

- Food, cake, people, packaging, or location is the first proof. Copy supports the image.
- Use real hands, staff, counters, rooms, boxes, labels, notes, and bakery work.
- Let one product or action dominate each frame.
- Open video on visible action: box opening, tray arriving, cake cutting, frosting, handoff, steam, or a location arrival.
- Build from full-bleed photography, then add one baby-blue or red copy surface inside the safe zone.
- Baby blue and logo red are the dominant system. White can support legibility. Cream and black are not leading brand fields in this launch pack.
- Keep the circular yum! mark active and visible as a real player, not a tiny corner watermark.
- Use straight cuts, restrained push-ins, and printed ticket/card motion. Avoid template-heavy transitions.
- No generated food, fake customers, fake packaging, stock celebrations, black CTA panels, pink washes, decorative gradients, or luxury black-and-gold styling.

## claim conflicts and release gates

- **Shipping/delivery:** the May 21 live-site capture says cakes did not ship or deliver, while the July 9 launch copy says Patticake ships nationwide. Use neutral `check current delivery or pickup options` language until the live checkout path and fulfillment rules are verified.
- **Patticake naming story:** the older structured profile marked `named after Patti Soskin` as unconfirmed; the newer July 9 approved copy bank marks it confirmed. The July 9 source is newer, but retain its owner-confirmation provenance if the claim is used publicly.
- **Dynamic details:** verify prices, hours, menu items, availability, deadlines, capacity, dietary claims, customization, and delivery coverage immediately before release.
- **Reviews:** aggregate ratings may change; individual reviews and photos require source capture and rights review before paid or promotional use.
- **Older color documentation:** older cream values and legacy neutral-first layouts are not the visual authority for this launch. The real baby-blue packaging and red circular mark control the current creative system.

## approved working pattern for this launch

1. Real photo or motion action.
2. One lowercase headline.
3. One short proof or operational cue.
4. One CTA.
5. Baby-blue and red brand framing with the yum! mark active.

This pattern replaces the earlier three-equal-text-box approach.
