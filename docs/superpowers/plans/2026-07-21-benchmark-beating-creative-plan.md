# Yum and Patticake benchmark-beating creative plan

Date: July 21, 2026

## Goal

Make yum! Kitchen and Patticake more distinctive, more useful, and easier to act on than the live yum! site, the current yum! social feed, Crumbl, Goldbelly, and SusieCakes without copying their visual language or weakening the brand.

The current foundation is strong: Patticake is the single hero product, nationwide availability is clear, real proof is rights-safe, baby blue and red lead, real photography stays unobstructed, and four launch films now cover five placements. This plan closes the remaining gaps in human immediacy, seasonal speed, and measured iteration.

## What winning means

| Reference | Their current advantage | How yum! and Patticake beat it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live yum! site | Current seasonal food imagery and immediate restaurant context. | Keep the same freshness while using a more ownable baby-blue and red hierarchy, clearer pickup and shipping actions, and no copy card over photography. |
| yum! social | Real people, guests, team members, neighborhood moments, and spontaneous voice. | Preserve that human mix, then add repeatable motion, packaging memory, and clear conversion posts as punctuation. |
| Crumbl | A fast weekly drop, one oversized product, one hook, and one action. | Build a same-day seasonal lane using current yum! photography, one lowercase hook, and one action while keeping yum!'s hospitality voice and real kitchen origin. |
| Goldbelly | Broad gifting utility, occasion depth, and aggressive offers. | Make one hero cake easier to understand and send, cover the most useful occasions, and keep the page calm. Never add a discount or urgency claim unless it is real and approved. |
| SusieCakes | Clear shipping, local pickup, founder story, ingredients, reviews, and customer celebration content. | Match the useful structure with a more distinctive bright-blue package, the real yum! bakery story, and proof that is fully sourced and rights-cleared. |

## Non-negotiable creative rules

- Bright baby blue and yum! red lead every designed asset.
- Real owned photography stays unobstructed.
- Copy uses a dedicated color field or a separate full-frame message scene.
- One idea and one action lead at a time.
- No stickers, tape, glow, shadowed type, corner lockups, equal three-box layouts, or decorative cards over photography.
- Patticake is available nationwide. Do not call it a new home or frame it as local-only.
- Patticake remains the single hero product. Other yum! cakes stay secondary.
- Motion closes on a readable action for at least two seconds.
- Every placement gets a reviewed focal crop. Faces, hero products, package marks, and required copy stay fully visible throughout motion.
- Review posters must use a stable photo or end-card frame, never a fade, wipe, or partial transition.
- Customer text, images, and video remain hidden until the rights ledger contains written approval.
- No invented menu items, prices, dates, delivery promises, ingredients, reviews, or promotions.

## Priority 1: build the weekly moment engine

### Deliverables

- One fast-turn template that accepts one approved current photo, one lowercase hook, one proof line, and one action.
- Five exports from the same source: 9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9, and a Story-safe still.
- Two versions of the motion master: an 8-second organic cut and a 10-second paid or launch cut.
- A review-board lane labeled `Current moment` that is visually separate from durable launch assets.
- A required expiration date in the source spec so seasonal work cannot remain active after the moment ends.

### Acceptance criteria

- A trained producer can move from an approved photo and copy line to all exports in one business day.
- Food or cake occupies at least 75 percent of product-led frames.
- The source spec fails validation when the photo, destination, approval owner, or expiration date is missing.
- No seasonal item is published unless it matches current menu or operations truth.
- The asset automatically leaves the active gallery after its expiration date but remains preserved in the archive.

## Priority 1: protect human social texture

Use designed assets as punctuation, not the entire feed.

Recommended nine-post mix:

- At least three real people, guest, team, or community moments.
- At least two food-first posts.
- One current local or seasonal moment.
- One Patticake occasion or bakery-origin post.
- No more than two designed conversion posts.

Every designed post must begin with a real moment, product, package, or person. Avoid generic motivational copy and trend language that could belong to another brand.

### Acceptance criteria

- The monthly feed review meets the mix above.
- Every customer or creator asset has a completed rights-ledger entry.
- Captions stay short, lowercase where appropriate, and specific to a person, dish, location, or occasion.
- Every conversion post has one destination. Community posts do not need a forced call to action.

## Priority 1: measure the commerce story

Track the current simplified commerce system before adding new interface choices.

Required events:

- Patticake hero action click.
- Nationwide order clickout.
- Local pickup click.
- Message-maker start and completion.
- Review-wall interaction.
- Secondary `more cakes from yum!` interaction.

Initial relative goals, to be reset after the first 30-day baseline:

- Improve Patticake hero click-through by 15 percent.
- Improve nationwide order clickout by 10 percent.
- Keep secondary-cake interaction from reducing Patticake clickout.
- Maintain zero serious or critical accessibility violations and Lighthouse SEO 100.

Do not add more choices when a copy, image, or proof change can improve the same metric.

## Priority 2: deepen proof without losing trust

- Continue dated aggregate Google, Yelp, and Restaurantji proof.
- Add individual customer text only after written reuse approval is recorded.
- Add real Instagram or TikTok posts only through the same approval gate.
- Capture founder, bakery, ingredient, package, and first-slice proof through owned production.
- Extend the strongest approved stories to Patticake, yum!, and relevant location pages without duplicating the same wall everywhere.

### Acceptance criteria

- Every individual proof item exposes source, approval status, approval evidence, approved channels, and expiration if applicable.
- The UI fails closed when any required rights field is missing.
- Proof remains current, linked, and dated.
- No sample review language returns.

## Priority 2: build occasion depth without marketplace clutter

Keep the current durable lanes:

- Birthday.
- Thank you.
- Send cake, not a card.
- Cake for the table.
- Local pickup.
- Bakery origin.

Add new occasion work only when it has a distinct photograph, message, and destination. Do not create a long category grid simply to match Goldbelly.

For any approved promotion:

- Record the exact offer, dates, eligibility, code, destination, and owner.
- Build one dominant offer frame, not an email-gate takeover.
- Remove or archive the creative automatically when the offer ends.

## Priority 3: keep motion tied to real moments

Use the current four-film launch system as the production model:

- Full-frame photograph.
- Separate baby-blue message scene.
- One red sentence.
- Short red cut.
- Real yum! logo player.
- Readable action close.

Next motion subjects should come from real business moments:

- A current seasonal dish entering the menu.
- A real cake packing and carrier handoff.
- A first nationwide delivery or recipient reaction with written rights.
- A team member or founder explaining the cake in one sentence.
- A restaurant event, neighborhood moment, or catering handoff.

Avoid producing motion only to fill a format. Every film needs a current event, product truth, person, or utility.

## Test and measurement cadence

### Every launch or campaign

1. Establish one primary action and one audience.
2. Select the strongest real source photograph or footage.
3. Produce the durable master and platform crops.
4. Review in `/asset-gallery` on desktop and mobile.
5. Validate copy, source, rights, destination, expiration, contrast, focal crop, poster frame, safe area, and reduced motion.
6. Publish one controlled version before expanding the set.

### Weekly

- Review the newest site and social moments.
- Pick one timely photo-led story.
- Compare hook retention, saves, shares, profile visits, and action clicks with the last ten comparable posts.
- Keep the winning structure, not merely the winning words.

### Monthly

- Refresh the live yum!, Crumbl, Goldbelly, and SusieCakes comparison.
- Score brand memory, human truth, product scale, offer clarity, proof, motion, and conversion.
- Archive expired creative.
- Select one improvement for the next production cycle.

## Ownership and required inputs

- Marketing chooses the current moment, audience, and primary action.
- Restaurant and bakery operations confirm product, availability, timing, and destination truth.
- The owner approves any offer, promotion, customer story, or creator reuse.
- Creative owns photography selection, copy hierarchy, motion, crops, and the gallery review set.
- Engineering owns tracking, fail-closed rights behavior, expiration handling, performance, and deployment verification.

If an input is missing, the asset stays in review. The production system must not guess.
