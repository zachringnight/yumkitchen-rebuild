# Yum Baby Blue Brand Pass Design

## Goal

Make the rebuilt Yum site and food-truck direction align more tightly with real Yum packaging: pale baby-blue packaging fields, warm white reading surfaces, strong red Yum marks, and dark ink only for typography.

## Source Evidence

- Gift boxes and to-go packaging use pale blue as the primary physical package color.
- The circular Yum logo is red with pale blue and white logo lettering.
- Menu PDFs use red for action/alerts and pale blue for textured supporting fields.
- Current generated food-truck concept is strongest where it reads like a Yum package on wheels: warm white body, baby-blue lower wrap, red mark/trim.

## Design Direction

Use baby blue as the brand field, not as a blanket page wash. The website should feel more Yum-owned through stronger blue header/footer and section bands while keeping food photography, ordering content, and long text on white or near-white surfaces. The food truck should stay warm white on the upper body for legibility, with a more dominant baby-blue lower wrap and red ribbon/awning/mark details.

## Implementation Scope

- Update global color tokens to use packaging-sampled blues.
- Strengthen header and footer blue treatments.
- Add a small reusable packaging-inspired visual system: blue fields, red ribbon rules, and warm paper surfaces.
- Apply those treatments to homepage and Patticake national delivery surfaces where the brand cue matters most.
- Create food-truck motion/rendering output as a separate artifact under `/Users/zsoskin/outputs`, not inside the web repo.

## Non-Goals

- Do not introduce fake or stock food photos.
- Do not replace real Yum photography.
- Do not make large black/dark panels.
- Do not redesign navigation, checkout, or form behavior in this pass.
